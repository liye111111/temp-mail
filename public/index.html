FAQ

Temporary email questions

What is a temporary email address?

It is a short-lived inbox you can use without sharing your personal email address. It is useful for low-risk registrations, trials and receiving verification messages.

Do I need to register?

No. GetOpenInbox creates an address immediately without asking for an account, password or personal email address.

How long does the inbox remain active?

The current inbox session is active for 10 minutes. Create a new address when the timer expires.

Should I use it for sensitive accounts?

No. Temporary email is not suitable for banking, healthcare, password recovery or any account you may need to recover later.
Temporary email content expires automatically from object storage. Do not use this service for sensitive information.
